    FAQ

    Aapke sawaal

    Kya normal phone camera kaafi hai?

    Haan. 720p ya usse upar ka video best rehta hai. Phone ko side-on, waist height par stable rakhein aur poora body frame mein aana chahiye.

    Analysis kitna accurate hai?

    Ye 2D pose estimation hai — ek camera se body points ka andaaza. Ye practice feedback ke liye useful hai, lekin lab-grade biomechanics ya bowling action legality test nahi hai. Har measurement ke saath hum source aur confidence dikhate hain.

    Mera video kahan jaata hai?

    Video ek short-lived signed link se seedha secure storage mein upload hota hai. Use sirf aap apne account se dekh sakte hain — koi dusra user access nahi kar sakta. Video file database mein nahi rakhi jaati.

    Kya ye real coach ki jagah le sakta hai?

    Nahi. CricVision aapke coach ka saathi hai — roz ki practice mein feedback deta hai, taaki coach ke saath waqt aur behtar use ho.

    Payment kaise hoga?

    Paid plans launch par UPI, card aur net banking ke saath aayenge. Abhi early access free hai.
